
1.  Department Overview

    -   Establishment year
	The Department of Information Technology was established in the year 2000.
	The Department of Information Technology was established in the year 2000 with B.E course.

    -   Affiliation details
	Affiliated to Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekananda Technical University, Bhilai.

    -   Accreditation details
	Accredited by All India Council of Technical Education, New Delhi.
	The four-year degree course is accredited by the All India Council for Technical Education and had earlier been affiliated with Pt. Ravishankar Shukla 	University since 2000, followed by affiliation to Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekananda University since 2005.

    -   Brief introduction
	Information Technology meets the hardware aspects of production with an in-depth knowledge of the software. The stretch of real-time requirements and technical 	know-how has created an immense requirement for professionals in the field of information technology. The course focuses on outpouring technical competence to 	students to work in the IT sector, to engage them in life-long learning, to make them communicate effectively, and to exhibit leadership skills.


2.  Vision Statement
	To achieve global standards of Education, Research and Innovation in Information Technology to meet the current challenges of modern computing industry.

3.  Mission Statements
	M1:To achieve Academic Excellence by providing need based quality training through Experienced Faculties in the field of Information Technology.
	M2:To maintain state-of-the-art facilities and laboratories for continuous upgradation by applying Pedagogical Tools for faculty and students.
	M3:To incorporate Research and Development activities through continuous learning based on Real-time Projects and Innovations.
	M4:To develop Entrepreneurship skills, Ethical values, and Extracurricular caliber for holistic development of students.

4.  Program Educational Objectives (PEO)
	PEO1:To enable graduates to employ Information Technology for ModernEngineering applications in context of Quality, Security, and Cost-effectiveness.
	PEO2:Able to correlate Basic Sciences with Engineering and apply the knowledge in Multi-disciplinary Technologies.
	PEO3:To prepare Graduates with Entrepreneurship skills, Research attributes and Motivation for higher studies.

5.  Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)
	PSO1 – The ability to analyze the problems and develop real time solutions using innovative methods and open-ended programming environments to deliver high 	quality product
	PSO2 – To demonstrate the knowledge of computer networks, communications, operating systems, and hardware technologies.
	PSO3 – The ability to pursue advanced studies and take the lead in innovation and entrepreneurship.
